System Design Timer

The 45-minute design round has a rhythm: clarify, sketch, deep-dive, scale, wrap up. This pacer keeps you on it — start it before your next practice round.

A pacer, not a prescription. Phase boundaries are a common practice structure, not a rule any interviewer enforces. Adapt the rhythm to the problem in front of you.

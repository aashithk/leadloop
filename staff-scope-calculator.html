Staff Scope Calculator

Are you operating at staff scope? Answer honestly across three dimensions — scope, influence, ambiguity — and get your Senior-vs-Staff readiness score plus a gap analysis.

Honest heuristic, not a verdict. This self-assessment reflects patterns commonly associated with staff-level work. It is not a prediction of any real promotion or hiring outcome — companies level differently, and only your own track record decides.

The 10 questions

For each statement, pick how true it is of your last 12 months. Be honest — the gap analysis is only as good as your answers.

1. My work regularly affects teams beyond my own.
2. I own services or systems end-to-end — on-call, runbooks, capacity.
3. Other teams adopt artifacts I created — libraries, standards, docs, talks.
4. I drive decisions in rooms where I have no formal authority.
5. My design feedback changes what other teams build.
6. I mentor engineers and can point to their concrete outcomes.
7. I resolve disagreements between teams without escalating.
8. I define the problem, not just execute someone else's spec.
9. I've led a project with no clear requirements to a shipped outcome.
10. I set technical direction that others follow.
